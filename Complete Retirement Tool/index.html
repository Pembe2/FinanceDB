Disclosures & Assumptions
Client-ready framing. Keep this in any PDF/report.
Important:
This tool provides educational projections based on user inputs. It does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice, does not recommend specific securities, and does not model individualized tax law, portfolio composition, or fees.
Modeling conventions
  • Annual time steps (end-of-year contributions; retirement withdrawals after growth).
  • “Today’s dollars” uses inflation discount over years-to-retirement.
  • RMD uses a simplified factor table starting at max(retirement age, 72).
Limitations
  • No Monte Carlo / sequence-of-returns modeling (deterministic scenarios only).
  • No tax modeling beyond optional spending estimator tax line item.
  • No portfolio fees, glidepaths, or asset allocation effects.
Portfolio Growth & Drawdown
